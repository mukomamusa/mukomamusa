## 🔴 CRITICAL - DEVELOPER MUST CREATE

### 1. Railway (Web Hosting)
- **Who creates:** Developer
- **Cost:** $5-20/month (starts at $5)
- **What it's for:** 
  - Hosting your website
  - Database storage
  - Automatic backups
- **Payment:** Credit card (monthly billing)
- **Why this:** Easiest and cheapest option

**What developer does:**
1. Creates Railway account
2. Connects to GitHub
3. Deploys your app
4. Sets up database
5. Configures everything

---

### 2. Flutterwave (Payment Gateway)
- **Who creates:** Developer (using your business details)
- **Cost:** 
  - FREE to sign up
  - 3.8% per transaction (only when you make money)
- **What it's for:**
  - Accept MTN Mobile Money
  - Accept Airtel Money
  - Accept Zamtel Kwacha
  - Accept card payments
- **Payment:** Deducted from each transaction
- **Why this:** Only payment gateway that works well in Zambia

**What developer does:**
1. Signs up with your business name
2. Completes KYC verification (needs your documents)
3. Gets API keys
4. Integrates into app
5. Tests with test transactions
6. Activates live mode

**Documents needed:**
- Business registration
- Your ID
- Bank account details
- Business address

---

### 3. Expo EAS (Mobile App Builds)
- **Who creates:** Developer
- **Cost:** 
  - FREE for testing
  - $29/month for production (recommended)
- **What it's for:**
  - Building iOS app
  - Building Android app
  - Push notifications
  - App updates
- **Payment:** Credit card (monthly)
- **Why this:** Required for mobile apps

**What developer does:**
1. Creates Expo account
2. Configures build settings
3. Builds iOS and Android apps
4. Submits to app stores

### 5. Africa's Talking (SMS Service)
- **Who creates:** Developer (using your business details)
- **Cost:**
  - ~K0.20 per SMS in Zambia
  - Start with K200-500 credit
- **What it's for:**
  - Booking confirmations via SMS
  - Payment confirmations
  - Reminders
- **Payment:** Prepaid credit (top up as needed)
- **Why this:** Best SMS service for Zambia

**What developer does:**
1. Creates account with your business name
2. Adds initial credit (K200-500)
3. Gets API key
4. Integrates SMS sending
5. Tests with Zambian numbers
