## Customer Guide

### 1. Searching for Buses

**Step 1:** Open the home page
- You'll see a search form with three fields

**Step 4:** Fill in your travel details
- **From:** Select your departure city (default: Lusaka)
- **To:** Select your destination city
- **Date:** Choose your travel date

**Step 3:** Enable Flexible Date Search (optional)
- Check "Flexible dates (±3 days)" to see buses within a week of your date
- A date carousel will appear showing buses available on nearby dates
- Click any date to filter results for that specific day
- See minimum prices and bus counts for each date

**Step 4:** Click "Search Buses"
- Available buses will be displayed below
- Each bus shows:
  - Company logo and name
  - Bus name and type
  - **Bus photo preview** (click to view gallery)
  - Departure and arrival times
  - Price per seat
  - Available seats with **color-coded indicator**:
    - 🟢 **Green:** Many seats (>10 available)
    - 🟡 **Yellow:** Limited seats (5-10 available)
    - 🔴 **Red:** Almost full (<5 available)
    - ⚫ **Gray:** Sold out
  - Amenities (AC, WiFi, etc.)
  - Intermediate stops
  - **Feature badges** highlighting special options:
    - 💰 **Lowest Price** - Best price available
    - 🎫 **Most Seats** - Most availability
    - ⭐ **VIP Coach** - Premium service
    - ⚡ **Express** - Direct/fast service

**Step 5:** Filter and sort results (optional)
- Click "Filters" button to expand filter options
- **Bus Type:** Filter by Standard Coach, Luxury, etc.
- **Min/Max Price:** Set price range in Kwacha
- **Time of Day:** Morning (5AM-12PM), Afternoon (12PM-5PM), Evening (5PM-5AM)
- **Amenities:** Click to filter by specific amenities:
  - 📶 WiFi
  - ❄️ AC (Air Conditioning)
  - 💺 Reclining Seats
  - 🔌 USB Charging
  - 🚻 Toilet
  - 🥤 Refreshments
  - 🎬 Entertainment
  - 🛏️ Blanket
- **Sort By:** Choose from:
  - Earliest Departure (default)
  - Price: Low to High
  - Price: High to Low
  - Most Seats Available
- A badge shows the number of active filters
- Click "Clear Filters" to reset all filters

### 2. Creating an Account

**Step 1:** Click "Customer Login" in the header

**Step 2:** Click "Don't have an account? Register"

**Step 3:** Fill in the registration form
- Full Name (e.g., John Mwansa)
- Phone Number (e.g., +260971234567)
- Email Address
- Password

**Step 4:** Click "Register"
- You'll see a success message
- The form will switch to login mode

**Step 5:** Login with your credentials

### 3. Booking a Bus Ticket

**Step 1:** Search for buses (see section 1)

**Step 2:** Click "Book Now" on your preferred bus
- If not logged in, you'll be redirected to login first

**Step 3:** Review trip details
- Verify the route, date, and times
- Check bus amenities

**Step 4:** Fill in booking information
- **Number of Seats:** How many seats you need (1-available seats)
- **Passenger Details:** For each passenger, enter:
  - Full name (as on ID)
  - NRC / ID Number
  - Phone number
  - Passenger type (Adult 18+, Child 2-17, Infant 0-2, Senior 60+)
- **Boarding Point:** Choose where you'll board
  - Origin city (e.g., Lusaka)
  - Or any intermediate stop (e.g., Kabwe)
- **Number of Luggage Pieces:** How many bags (0-5)

**Step 5:** Review booking summary
- Check total price
- Verify all details

**Step 6:** Click "Confirm Booking"
- Wait for processing
- You'll see a confirmation screen

**Step 7:** Save your booking reference
- A unique reference number is generated (e.g., BK1A2B3C4D5E)
- Your seat numbers are assigned automatically
- Total price is displayed

### 4. Viewing Your Bookings

**Step 1:** Login to your account

**Step 2:** You'll be on the Customer Dashboard

**Step 3:** Scroll down to "My Bookings" section
- All your bookings are listed
- Each booking shows:
  - Booking reference number
  - Route (origin → destination)
  - Date and times
  - Bus and company details
  - Seat numbers
  - Boarding point
  - Luggage count
  - Total price
  - Status (Confirmed/Cancelled/Completed)

### 5. Cancelling a Booking

**Step 1:** Go to your Customer Dashboard

**Step 2:** Find the booking you want to cancel

**Step 3:** Click the "Cancel Booking" button (red button on the left)
- Note: This button only appears for **pending** or **confirmed** bookings
- **Completed** trips cannot be cancelled

**Step 4:** Confirm the cancellation in the popup dialog
- Click "OK" to confirm
- Click "Cancel" to keep your booking

**Step 5:** Your booking is cancelled
- Status changes to "Cancelled"
- Seats are automatically restored and available for others
- Ticket becomes invalid
- Refund is calculated based on cancellation policy (see below)

**Cancellation Restrictions:**
- ❌ **Completed bookings** cannot be cancelled (trip already taken)
- ❌ **Already cancelled** bookings cannot be cancelled again
- ⚠️ Refund amount depends on how far in advance you cancel

**Note:** Cancellations cannot be undone. Please be certain before cancelling.

### 6. Important Tips for Customers

✅ **Arrive Early:** Get to the boarding point 15 minutes before departure

✅ **Save Your Reference:** Keep your booking reference number safe

✅ **Check Boarding Point:** Make sure you know where to board

✅ **Luggage Limits:** Standard luggage allowance applies

✅ **Contact Information:** Keep your phone number updated

## Bus Company Guide

### 1. Registering Your Company

**Step 1:** Click "Company Login" in the header

**Step 2:** Click "Don't have an account? Register"

**Step 3:** Fill in company registration form
- **Company Name:** Your bus company name
- **License Number:** Your operating license number
- **Contact Person Name:** Manager or owner name
- **Phone Number:** Company contact number
- **Email Address:** Company email
- **Password:** Secure password

**Step 4:** Click "Register"
- Success message will appear
- Switch to login mode

**Step 5:** Login with your credentials

### 1a. Uploading Company Logo

Brand your company on search results!

**Step 1:** Login to Company Dashboard

**Step 2:** Go to "Settings/Profile" tab

**Step 3:** Find the "Company Logo" section
- Shows current logo or placeholder
- Supported formats: JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF
- Maximum file size: 2MB

**Step 4:** Click "Upload Logo" button
- Select your company logo image
- Logo uploads immediately
- Preview appears in the logo section

**Step 5:** Logo appears in search results
- Customers see your logo next to company name
- Builds brand recognition and trust

**Tips for Company Logo:**
- ✅ Use a square or nearly square image
- ✅ Ensure logo is clear at small sizes (40x40px)
- ✅ Use your official company logo
- ✅ Transparent backgrounds work well

### 2. Adding Buses to Your Fleet

**Step 1:** Login to Company Dashboard

**Step 2:** You'll see the "My Buses" tab (default view)

**Step 3:** Click "+ Add Bus" button

**Step 4:** Fill in bus details
- **Bus Number:** Registration number (e.g., ZM-001-LK)
- **Bus Name:** Descriptive name (e.g., Express Coach)
- **Total Seats:** Number of passenger seats (20-60)
- **Bus Type:** Choose from:
  - Standard Coach
  - Luxury Coach
  - VIP Coach
- **Amenities:** Comma-separated list (e.g., AC,WiFi,Reclining Seats,USB Charging)

**Step 5:** Click "Create Bus"
- Bus will appear in your fleet list
- You can now create routes for this bus

### 2a. Adding Bus Photos

Showcase your buses to attract more customers!

**Step 1:** Go to "Buses" tab

**Step 2:** Find your bus and click the "📷 Photos" button

**Step 3:** The Bus Photo Gallery modal opens
- Shows current photos (0/5 images)
- Displays supported formats: JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF
- Maximum file size: 2MB per image

**Step 4:** Click "Add Photos" to upload
- Select one or multiple images
- Images upload automatically
- You can upload up to 5 images per bus

**Step 5:** Manage your photos
- Hover over an image to see the delete button
- Click an image to view in fullscreen lightbox
- Photos appear in search results to attract customers

**Tips for Bus Photos:**
- ✅ Include exterior shots (shows bus condition)
- ✅ Include interior shots (shows seating comfort)
- ✅ Show amenities (screens, USB ports, reclining seats)
- ✅ Use well-lit, clear photos
- 📸 Recommended: 1 exterior, 2-3 interior, 1 amenity close-up

### 3. Creating Routes

**Step 1:** Go to "Routes" tab

**Step 2:** Click "+ Add Route" button
- Note: You must have at least one bus registered first

**Step 3:** Fill in route details
- **Select Bus:** Choose from your fleet
- **Date:** Select travel date (today or future)
- **Origin:** Departure city (default: Lusaka)
- **Destination:** Arrival city
- **Departure Time:** When the bus leaves (e.g., 06:00)
- **Arrival Time:** When the bus arrives (e.g., 10:30)
- **Price (ZMW):** Ticket price per seat (minimum: 50)
- **Intermediate Stops:** Optional, comma-separated (e.g., Kabwe,Kapiri Mposhi)

**Step 4:** Click "Create Route"
- Route will be created with all seats available (from bus capacity)
- Customers can now see and book this route

### 3a. Duplicating Routes

Quickly create copies of existing routes:

**Step 1:** Go to "Routes & Schedules" tab

**Step 2:** Find an existing route you want to copy

**Step 3:** Click the "Duplicate" button (purple)

**Step 4:** Choose schedule type:
- **Single Date:** Create one copy for a specific date
- **Recurring:** Create multiple routes at once

**Step 5:** For Single Date:
- Select the new date
- Optionally modify departure time, arrival time, or price
- Click "Create Route"

**Step 6:** For Recurring Routes:
- Choose recurrence pattern:
  - **Daily:** Every day in date range
  - **Weekdays:** Monday to Friday only
  - **Weekends:** Saturday and Sunday only
  - **Weekly:** Select specific days (Mon, Tue, etc.)
- Set start and end dates
- Optionally modify times or price
- Click "Create Routes"

**Tips for Recurring Routes:**
- ✅ Great for regular scheduled services
- ✅ Saves time vs creating routes individually
- ⚠️ Dates with conflicts (same bus/time) are automatically skipped
- 📊 Summary shows how many routes were created/skipped

### 4. Managing Bookings

**Step 4:** View the complete passenger list

### 6. Managing Route Status

**Step 1:** Go to "Routes" tab in the dashboard

**Step 2:** Find the route you want to update

**Step 3:** Use the status buttons on each route card:

**For Active Routes:**
- Click "Mark Departed" when the bus leaves
- Status changes to "Departed" (blue badge)

**For Departed Routes:**
- Click "Mark Completed" when the trip ends
- Status changes to "Completed" (green badge)

**Status Types:**
| Status | Description | Badge Color |
|--------|-------------|-------------|
| Active | Route is scheduled and accepting bookings | Gray |
| Departed | Bus has left the origin | Blue |
| Completed | Trip has finished | Green |
| Cancelled | Route was cancelled | Red |
| Delayed | Route is running late | Yellow |

**Automatic Route Completion:**
- Routes are **automatically marked as completed** when their travel date passes
- This happens when you load the dashboard
- Associated bookings are also marked as completed
- No manual action required for past routes

### 7. Dashboard Statistics

The dashboard shows three key metrics:
- **Total Buses:** Number of buses in your fleet
- **Active Routes:** Number of scheduled routes
- **Total Bookings:** Number of customer bookings

### 8. Managing Bus Subscriptions

Each bus requires an active subscription to have its routes visible to customers.

**Understanding Subscription Status:**

| Status | Description | Routes Visible? |
|--------|-------------|----------------|
| Trial | New buses get a 14-day free trial | ✅ Yes |
| Active | Paid subscription is active | ✅ Yes |
| Expired | Subscription has ended | ❌ No (hidden) |

**Viewing Subscription Status:**

**Step 1:** Go to "Subscriptions" tab in the dashboard

**Step 2:** View the subscription summary:
- Total buses in your fleet
- Active subscriptions count
- Buses on trial
- Expired subscriptions needing renewal

**Step 3:** Each bus card shows:
- Current subscription status (Trial/Active/Expired)
- Days remaining until expiration
- Expiration date

**Subscribing or Renewing a Bus:**

**Step 1:** Go to "Subscriptions" tab

**Step 2:** Find the bus you want to subscribe

**Step 3:** Click the appropriate button:
- **🚀 Subscribe** - For buses on trial
- **🔄 Renew Now** - For expired buses
- **➕ Extend** - For active buses

**Step 4:** Review the subscription details:
- Bus name and number
- Monthly price (e.g., K500/month)
- Current status and what will happen

**Step 5:** Click "💳 Pay Now" to confirm

**Step 6:** Payment confirmation:
- Subscription activates immediately
- Routes become visible to customers
- 1 month is added to subscription period

**Payment History:**

Scroll down in the Subscriptions tab to see:
- All past payments
- Payment dates
- Amounts paid
- Subscription periods covered

**Tips:**
- ⚠️ Renew before expiration to avoid route visibility gaps
- 📅 Subscribe during trial to lock in your buses
- 💰 Monthly due is calculated for all expired buses

### 9. Earnings Overview

The dashboard shows your financial performance:

**Earnings Card (at top of dashboard):**
- **Gross Revenue:** Total from all bookings
- **Platform Commission:** 7.5% fee on bookings
- **Net Earnings:** Your take-home amount

**Formula:** Net Earnings = Gross Revenue - (Gross Revenue × 7.5%)

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues and Solutions

#### Cannot Login
- **Check credentials:** Ensure email and password are correct
- **Case sensitive:** Passwords are case-sensitive
- **Account type:** Use correct login page (Customer vs Company)

#### No Buses Found
- **Check dates:** Ensure you're searching for future dates
- **Try different routes:** Not all routes may be available daily
- **Broaden search:** Try searching without destination first

#### Booking Failed
- **Seats unavailable:** Someone may have booked before you
- **Refresh page:** Try searching again for updated availability
- **Check details:** Ensure all required fields are filled

#### Cannot Add Route
- **Add bus first:** You must register at least one bus
- **Check date:** Date must be today or in the future
- **Valid times:** Ensure departure time is before arrival time
